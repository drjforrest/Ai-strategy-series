# Rhetoric vs Readiness Quadrant (2024)

![[rhetoric_vs_readiness_quadrant_2024.png]]

Scatter figure for the HI Africa work: **rhetoric** (text-derived emphasis, z-scored within dimension) on the x-axis against **readiness** (measured Oxford AI Readiness score, z-scored within dimension) on the y-axis, by country and dimension, measured year 2024. Pooled r = 0.13 (weak alignment between what countries say and where they are).

## Key points
- Quadrant labels: "walk > talk" (upper left), "aligned (high)" (upper right), "talk > walk" (lower right).
- Series coloured by dimension group: Data and Infrastructure, Government, Technology Sector.
- Labelled outliers include Cote d'Ivoire (Vision), Benin (Digital Capacity), Zimbabwe (Maturity; Governance and Ethics), Morocco (Maturity), Egypt (Adaptability).
- Weak pooled correlation supports the narrative that national AI/digital-health rhetoric is often decoupled from measured readiness.
