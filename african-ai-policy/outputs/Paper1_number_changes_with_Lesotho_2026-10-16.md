## Interpretation change
The readiness result is no longer "near-threshold" (p = 0.056 depended on the 15-country set). With Lesotho it is p = 0.167, and no sensitivity or leave-one-out variant reaches 0.05. Wording such as "approached significance", "near-threshold full-sample result" and "the separation depends on South Africa" should become a plain null: "no detectable association in any specification". The abstract sentence "three leave-one-country-out runs reached p ≤ 0.05" must be removed.
